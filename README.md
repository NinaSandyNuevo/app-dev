# Four Sisters and a Wedding

## Overview
*Four Sisters and a Wedding* is a **Filipino family drama-comedy film** directed by **Cathy Garcia-Molina** and released in 2013.  
It tells the story of four sisters who reunite to stop their youngest brother’s wedding, leading to emotional revelations and heartfelt moments about love, family, and forgiveness.

### Main Cast
1. Bea Alonzo as **Bobbie Salazar**
2. Toni Gonzaga as **Teddie Salazar**
3. Angel Locsin as **Alex Salazar**
4. Shaina Magdayao as **Gabbie Salazar**
5. Enchong Dee as **CJ Salazar**

### Why I Like It
This movie shows the importance of **family**, *understanding*, and **forgiveness**.  
It mixes drama and comedy in a way that feels real and touching.

### My Favorite Moments
- The sisters arguing and reconciling in an emotional scene  
- The wedding preparations full of chaos and laughter  
- Bobbie’s iconic line: **“You always think you’re the star!”**

### Fun Fact
*Four Sisters and a Wedding* is one of the most **beloved Filipino family films** and remains popular years after its release.
